# Research: what parents need from planning to age 9

Desk research, Nov 14 2026. Each claim links its source. Items marked (verify) came from secondary sources or could not be opened at the primary page; check them before they go into the deck or into app logic.

## Who we serve

Affluent US couples, 25 to 45, both working, who can pay for help but have no time to figure out what they should be thinking about at each stage. They already spend heavily on outsourced care: a nanny averages $827 a week ([Care.com 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250129569000/en)), a night nurse runs about $35 to $50 an hour ([Babylist](https://www.babylist.com/hello-baby/what-is-a-night-nurse)), a sleep consultant package has a median of about $450 ([Huckleberry 2026](https://huckleberrycare.com/blog/how-much-does-a-baby-sleep-consultant-cost)). What they lack is one place that knows their situation and tells each parent what matters this week.

## Pain points with evidence

| Pain | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Maternal mental health | 13.2% of mothers reported postpartum depressive symptoms, 9.7% to 23.5% by state ([CDC MMWR 2020](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/69/wr/mm6919a2.htm)). Mental health conditions are a leading cause of pregnancy-related death, and over 80% of those deaths are preventable ([CDC 2022](https://www.cdc.gov/media/releases/2022/p0919-pregnancy-related-deaths.html)). |
| Fathers are invisible | Paternal perinatal depression is about 10%, peaking at 25.6% at 3 to 6 months postpartum, and correlates with the mother's depression ([Paulson, JAMA 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20483973/)). Most products target mothers only. |
| The couple drifts apart | Relationship satisfaction declines for both partners from pregnancy to 12 months postpartum, and further to 24 months ([meta-analysis of 49 studies, 2022](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.901362/pdf)). |
| Sleep loss is unequal and long | Mothers lose about 62 minutes a night and fathers 13 at the low point; neither fully recovers within 6 years ([Richter et al., Sleep 2019](https://wrap.warwick.ac.uk/113259)). |
| Parents are overwhelmed | 48% of parents say most days they are completely overwhelmed ([Surgeon General advisory 2024](https://kvia.com/health/2024/08/29/parental-stress-is-a-significant-public-health-issue-surgeon-general-says-in-new-advisory-3/), verify on hhs.gov). |
| Children's mental health | 11% of children 3 to 17 have diagnosed anxiety; 21% have ever had a mental, emotional or behavioral diagnosis ([CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/children-mental-health/data-research/index.html)). Autism is about 1 in 31 at age 8 ([CDC ADDM 2025](https://www.disabilityscoop.com/2025/04/16/cdc-raises-autism-prevalence-estimate/31413/)). |
| Deadlines are easy to miss | Nearly half of US pregnancies are unplanned, so folic acid often starts late ([USPSTF](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/folic-acid-for-the-prevention-of-neural-tube-defects-preventive-medication)). Screenings have narrow windows: glucose at 24 to 28 weeks, Tdap at 27 to 36, GBS at 36 to 37, autism screening at 18 and 24 months. |
| Guidance keeps changing | The CDC childhood vaccine schedule was cut in Jan 2026 and the change was stayed in court in Mar 2026; the AAP keeps its own schedule ([CRS](https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/IN12684.html)). The AAP replaced fixed screen-time limits in Jan 2026 ([AAP](https://www.aap.org/en/patient-care/media-and-children/center-of-excellence-on-social-media-and-youth-mental-health/understanding-the-new-AAP-digital-media-guidelines/)). Parents cannot track this alone. |

## The journey, stage by stage

Each stage lists what the family needs, what the app stores, and examples of personalized triggers. Triggers are the core of the product: data in, the right action out, for the right parent.

### 1. Planning (preconception)

- Needs: preconception visit; folic acid 400 to 800 mcg daily at least a month before conception ([USPSTF](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/folic-acid-for-the-prevention-of-neural-tube-defects-preventive-medication)); medication review (do not stop psychiatric medication alone); carrier screening, ideally before pregnancy (verify ACOG CO 690); insurance and leave check; the couple aligns on timing, money and division of labor; the partner's own health review.
- Data: medications, conditions, family history, vaccines, carrier results, supplement adherence, cycle data.
- Triggers: planning and no folate logged, suggest starting now. A medication that is a folate antagonist (methotrexate, valproic acid, carbamazepine), suggest talking to the prescriber first.

### 2. Trying to conceive and fertility

- Needs: see a specialist after 12 months of trying under 35, after 6 months at 35 or older, sooner over 40 ([ASRM 2026](https://www.asrm.org/practice-guidance/practice-committee-documents/fertility-evaluation-of-infertile-women-a-committee-opinion-2026/)); semen analysis early, since male factor contributes in up to 40% of cases; IVF costs $12k to $25k per cycle ([HHS 2024](https://www.hhs.gov/about/news/2024/03/13/fact-sheet-in-vitro-fertilization-ivf-use-across-united-states.html)); stress and grief support.
- Triggers: partner 35 or older and trying for 6 months, suggest a fertility evaluation and generate a records summary. Two weeks of low mood during treatment, offer a therapist.

### 3. Pregnancy

- Needs: first prenatal visit around 8 to 10 weeks; visits every 4 weeks to week 28, every 2 to week 36, then weekly (verify ACOG); anatomy scan 18 to 22 weeks; glucose screen 24 to 28 weeks; Tdap 27 to 36 weeks; GBS 36 to 37 weeks; choose pediatrician, birth class, leave plan, postpartum plan; the partner learns warning signs and prepares for the first nights.
- Mental health: antenatal depression and anxiety symptoms are common (verify the 2023 meta-analysis figures); prior depression is the strongest signal to plan postpartum support early. USPSTF recommends counseling for people at increased risk of perinatal depression ([USPSTF 2019](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/perinatal-depression-preventive-interventions)).
- Data: due date, labs and results, blood pressure and weight, ultrasound dates, mood check-ins, birth preferences, care team.
- Triggers: week 24 and no glucose result, schedule it. Week 34 and no Tdap, the window closes at 36. Prior depression on file, build a postpartum mental health plan and therapist shortlist now. Due in 8 weeks and no pediatrician, choose one.

### 4. Birth and the first 6 weeks

- Needs: contact with the mother's provider within 3 weeks and a full postpartum visit by 12 weeks (ACOG CO 736, verify); urgent maternal warning signs ([CDC Hear Her](https://www.cdc.gov/hearher/maternal-warning-signs/index.html)); safe sleep: back, firm flat surface, room-share without bed-sharing (AAP 2022, verify); newborn visits at 3 to 5 days, 2 weeks, 1 month; postpartum depression screening of the caregiver at the 1, 2, 4 and 6 month well-baby visits ([AAP periodicity](https://www.aap.org/en/practice-management/care-delivery-approaches/periodicity-schedule/)).
- Data: feeding, sleep and diaper logs, maternal recovery and vitals, mood screen for each parent, appointments.
- Triggers: a warning-sign symptom logged shows a fixed urgent screen (call 911 or the OB), never an AI answer. Mood dropping for 2 weeks or a positive self-harm item shows the National Maternal Mental Health Hotline 1-833-852-6262 and the PSI HelpLine 1-800-944-4773 ([PSI](https://www.postpartum.net/get-help/help-for-dads/)).

### 5. Infancy, 2 to 12 months

- Needs: vaccines at 2, 4, 6 and 12 months (show the AAP schedule and its source, since the CDC schedule is in flux); CDC milestone checklists at 2, 4, 6, 9 and 12 months ([CDC Act Early](https://www.cdc.gov/act-early/milestones/index.html)); developmental screening at 9 months; early peanut introduction for high-risk infants at 4 to 6 months ([NIAID](https://www.niaid.nih.gov/sites/default/files/addendum-peanut-allergy-prevention-guidelines.pdf)); return to work, childcare (average $13,128 a year in 2024, [Child Care Aware](https://info.childcareaware.org/media/new-findings-child-care-prices-continue-to-rise-as-supply-remains-stagnant)); pelvic floor recovery; the couple rebalances the load.
- Triggers: back to work in 6 weeks with no childcare booked, show a shortlist and cost. One parent sleeping under 5 hours for 5 nights while the other sleeps more, propose a shift swap and offer night help. A 9-month milestone marked "not yet", show the CDC guidance and early intervention referral, without diagnosing.

### 6. Toddler, 1 to 3

- Needs: well visits at 12, 15, 18, 24 and 30 months; developmental screening at 18 and 30 months and autism screening at 18 and 24 ([AAP 2020](https://fhop.ucsf.edu/sites/g/files/tkssra4481/f/periodicity_schedule_-_UPDATED_2020.pdf)); 11 to 14 hours of sleep ([AASM](https://depts.washington.edu/dbpeds/pediatricsleepdurationconsensus.pdf)); dental home by age 1 (verify AAPD); tantrums, language, serve-and-return interaction ([Harvard Center on the Developing Child](https://developingchild.harvard.edu/)); daycare or nanny; screens.
- Data: growth, vaccines, allergens and reactions, screening results, milestones, sleep, vocabulary, both parents' mood.
- Triggers: 17 months and no M-CHAT logged, ask for it at the 18-month visit. Short sleep three nights running and both parents low, propose a split night plan.

### 7. Preschool, 3 to 5

- Needs: 10 to 13 hours of sleep; active play through the day; vision screening (verify); boosters at 4 to 6 years (verify, schedule in flux); emotion regulation, peer play, early anxiety signs; preschool and kindergarten decisions.
- Evidence-based parenting help for behavior problems: PCIT (meta-analysis of 100 studies, large effect, [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34668468/)) and Triple P.
- Triggers: turning 4, remind about boosters, vision check and the district's kindergarten enrollment calendar. Behavior incidents trending up for 2 weeks, offer a parent training program.

### 8. Early school age, 5 to 9

- Needs: 9 to 12 hours of sleep; 60 minutes of activity a day; annual well visit; reading by grade 3; anxiety, ADHD, friendships, bullying; school choice, tutoring, first devices; parents' homework and logistics load.
- Data: report cards, reading level, teacher notes, IEP or 504 status, activities, device rules, mood and anxiety checks, therapist notes.
- Triggers: grade 2 reading flag from a teacher, guide the parents to request an evaluation. A parent's diary keeps returning to school refusal, offer an anxiety screener and a child therapist.

## Paid services the ecosystem can connect

By stage: fertility consults and genetic counseling; perinatal and couples therapy (including for fathers); doulas; childbirth and partner classes; lactation consultants; night nurses and postpartum doulas; pelvic floor physical therapy; sleep consultants; pediatric concierge; childcare matching; speech and occupational therapy; child therapists; parent coaching (PCIT, Triple P); tutors and reading specialists.

## Market

- 3.6M US births in 2025 ([CDC NCHS](https://cdc.gov/nchs/pressroom/releases/20260409.html)). 32.9M families with children under 18 ([BLS 2026](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/famee_04232026.htm)). 33.8% of US families earn over $150k (Census F-23, verify the share for families with young children in ACS B19131).
- Competitors:
  - Maven Clinic: the closest whole-journey product, but it is sold to employers and centered on women. It reportedly has about $268M ARR at a $1.7B valuation ([Sacra estimate](https://sacra.com/c/maven-clinic), [Fierce 2024](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/finance/maven-clinic-clinches-125m-invest-tech-value-based-care)).
  - Huckleberry: infant sleep and tracking, about $120 a year ([pricing](https://huckleberrycare.com/pricing)).
  - Kinedu: child development, up to $99 a month with coaching.
  - Lovevery: play kits, over $100M in subscription ARR ([Retail Dive](https://www.retaildive.com/news/childrens-company-lovevery-raises-100m-launches-mobile-app/609220)).
  - Babylist: a registry and commerce business with over $750M revenue in 2025.
  - Summer Health: pediatric care by text, about $20 a month.
- Gap: in what we found, no B2C product covers the whole journey from planning to age 9, for both parents, on the family's own data, with personalized guidance. Not checked yet: Nanit, Tinyhood, Oath Care, Juniper, Mae. Check them before the deck says "nobody".
- Pricing signal: consumer health memberships range from $199 a year (One Medical) to about $1,500 to $3,300 a year (Parsley Health). A blended ARPU of $250 to $600 a year means $100M ARR at roughly 170k to 400k paying families. That is about 5% to 11% of one year's births, and families stay for nine years.
- Retention: the median annual renewal for health apps is 54% ([RevenueCat 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/average-subscription-renewal-rates-by-app-category/)). Parenting needs change by stage, so the product has to earn its renewal at every stage. A journey to age 9 is the answer to stage churn, if the product really carries over.

## Trust, safety and regulation

- HIPAA usually does not cover a direct-to-consumer app. The FTC Health Breach Notification Rule (2024 update) does, and so does the FTC Act on deceptive data sharing; the GoodRx and BetterHelp cases are the precedent. Washington's My Health My Data Act requires separate consent to collect and to share, and lets users sue.
- FDA: position the product as wellness, planning and education. If the AI interprets lab results or recommends treatment, the product starts to look like a medical device. The AI explains, reminds, prepares questions for the doctor and routes to professionals. It does not diagnose.
- Product rules that follow from this:
  - Diaries and therapy notes are private to their author by default. Neither the partner nor the AI sees them without an explicit opt-in per item.
  - No ad-tech SDKs and no data sale.
  - Crisis paths (warning signs, self-harm) are fixed screens with hotlines, never generated text.
  - Every health rule shows its source and the date it was checked, because guidance changes (see the vaccine schedule above).
- Therapists and doctors need state licensure (PSYPACT covers about 40 jurisdictions). Work through partner networks rather than employing clinicians.
