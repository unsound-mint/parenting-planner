## Conflicts and changes

- Postpartum Tdap: current CDC (page dated Dec 2, 2025) recommends it only for people who have never had Tdap. Older CDC and Immunize.org material from 2012 to 2013 advised a postpartum dose for anyone not vaccinated in pregnancy, and gave a before-discharge timing. Only the snippet of the 2012 CDC release was seen: [CDC 2012 Tdap release](https://www.cdc.gov/media/releases/2012/a1024_Tdap_immunization.html) (verify). Use the current CDC rule.
- Gestational diabetes postpartum window: ADA 2026 says 4 to 12 weeks (opened, recommendation 15.30). An IHS summary of the 2026 Standards and a 2012 AFP summary say 6 to 12 weeks: [IHS: Preconception, Pregnancy and Postpartum Diabetes](https://www.ihs.gov/diabetes/clinician-resources/soc/preconception-pregnancy-postpartum-diabetes1/) (verify), [AFP 2012: ADA Releases Revisions](https://www.aafp.org/afp/2012/0301/p514) (verify). Use ADA's 4 to 12 weeks, and recheck against the ADA text.
- Breastfeeding duration: CDC's recommendations page says continue to 2 years or older (opened). CDC's infant and toddler nutrition page, seen only in search, cites the 2020 to 2025 Dietary Guidelines as continuing to 12 months or older: [CDC: Infant and Toddler Nutrition, Breastfeeding](https://cdc.gov/infant-toddler-nutrition/breastfeeding/index.html) (verify). Show both and cite the year.
- Marketplace versus employer enrollment after birth: the brief's 30-day window applies to employer group plans (DOL, opened). HealthCare.gov lists 60 days for the marketplace special enrollment period (opened). Keep both, keyed by plan type.
- Postpartum blood pressure timing: secondary sources cite 72 hours for severe-range readings and 7 to 10 days for other hypertensive disorders. ACOG's primary text could not be opened (HTTP 402), so both rows stay (verify).
- Hotline numbers: research.md lists the National Maternal Mental Health Hotline at 1-833-852-6262. The PSI page opened here does not list that hotline, so it is not verified. The PSI HelpLine, 1-800-944-4773, is confirmed.
- Paternal depression peak: research.md cites 25.6 percent at 3 to 6 months from Paulson, JAMA 2010. PubMed returned a cookie wall, so the figure stays unverified. This file states only the timing.
- FMLA hours threshold: research.md left it open. DOL Fact Sheet 28Q confirms 1,250 hours in the 12 months before leave.
- CDC contraception, postpartum: the 2024 SPR text opened covers copper IUD and implant timing. Injectable and pill postpartum timing was not visible in the text read, so no rows cover them. Interpregnancy interval was not stated in the SPR text read.
- AAP caregiver screening: research.md lists 1, 2, 4 and 6 months. The AAP change summary confirms these visits, but the schedule table itself was not read (the row for months 6 to 12 is marked verify).
