# Product

Owner: Dayana. Engineers build what is in "MVP"; anything else needs to move here first.

## Problem and user

Couples in the US expecting their first baby. Preparation is uneven: one parent (usually mom) carries the research, appointments and the mental load, and the partner arrives at the first night home without practice or a clear role. After birth, night care is split ad hoc and information gets lost between shifts ("when did she last eat?").

## What we sell

Each parent gets their own screen, and a shared family plan connects them. Preparation turns into real care: the partner rehearses the first night in a simulator, commits to an evening shift, and that shift is in the schedule when the baby comes home. Handoffs carry the last feeding, what is done and what is next.

Competitors to position against: Pregnancy+ (pregnancy tracking), Huckleberry (sleep and feeding tracker), Maven (family health benefit, sold to employers). None of them is built around two parents splitting and handing off care.

## MVP (the demo loop)

1. Landing page, one click into a demo family. No sign-up.
2. Two parents, Mom and Partner, each with a "Today" screen, shown side by side.
3. Partner completes the "First night at home" training and takes the evening shift.
4. Stage switch from pregnancy to "baby is home". Shifts agreed earlier appear in the schedule.
5. One parent logs a feeding and hands off. The other accepts and sees last feeding, next task and the baby card.
6. Pricing page showing what is free and what is paid.

## Out of scope for the hackathon

Real accounts and login, calendar sync (Google, Apple, Outlook), insurance and clinic search, psychologist booking, diaries and pattern detection, Happy Box and physical products, kindergarten stage, push notifications, payments.
