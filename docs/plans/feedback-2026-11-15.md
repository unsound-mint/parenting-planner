# Feedback pass, Nov 15 (night before freeze)

Denis's review of the live site, 25 screenshots. The pattern behind the items:

- No medical claims in our own voice. Say what a named guideline says, phrase it as guidance, and always point to the doctor. No dosages or numbers stated as advice.
- Sources are quiet inline links in the sentence, never a footer list. No "checked YYYY-MM-DD" in the UI (the date stays in the rule data). Not every card needs a source.
- Everything shown must be editable and work end to end from the database. No half features: upgrade or drop.
- One fact once per screen, clear hierarchy, structure over walls of chips. Graphs where data has a shape.
- Say "your partner" (or the member's own name from data), never a hardcoded demo name in copy.
- Explanations ("Why") read as a reason ("because ..."), not as an order.
- Content must change with the stage and the week.
