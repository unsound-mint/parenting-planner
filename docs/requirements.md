# Contest requirements

Business Cup 2.0 by Zimran, "From zero to product". Source: organizer slides, Nov 14 2026.

## The challenge

Build a web product that closes a fundamental pain for adults in the US, one they will pay for.

- Audience: US, ages 25 to 45. Working adults with jobs, families, bills and goals.
- Model: B2C. We sell to people, not companies.
- Platform: web only, runs in the browser. No mobile apps.
- Time: 24 hours, from idea to working product.

Tracks (pick one, then narrow to one user and one pain): Money, Entertainment, Skills. We position under **Skills**: parents learning newborn care and how to split it, through practice instead of reading.

## Scoring

| Weight | Question | What judges look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 40% | Does it work? | Works end to end in production, not a mockup. Core value is real, not faked or hardcoded. Quality of UX and code; they read the repo. |
| 20% | What do we sell? | Clear what the user pays for and why they come back tomorrow. |
| 15% | Who do we sell to? | Clear choice of problem and deep understanding of the target user. |
| 15% | Why does it make money? | Big or growing niche, $100M ARR long term, LTV > CAC. |
| 10% | Is it new? | Not a copy of a business that already exists. |

Pass/fail: B2C only, web only, all three submission items present.

## Submission

Deadline **12:00, Nov 15**. Resubmit as often as you like; the latest version is reviewed. One submission per team, by the captain.

1. Pitch deck, PDF, up to 10 slides, covering the criteria above.
2. Production link: a live product the jury can use end to end. No sign-up or login.
3. GitHub repo: public, no access requests.

Missing any of the three makes the submission incomplete.
