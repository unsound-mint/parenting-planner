# Demo script (draft, Dayana to finalize)

The acceptance test for the hackathon scope in `product.md`. Every step must work on the production link with a fresh demo family. Target length: 2 minutes.

1. Open the site and pick the "2nd trimester" family. Mom's screen shows her week, what is due (glucose screen at 24 to 28 weeks, with its source), and a plan built from her own data: each item names the data and the guideline that put it there, with a source link, and her sleep and mood from the last few days sit beside it.
2. Switch to the Partner. His plan is different: Tdap timing for him, a partner class, and a note that he has not done a mood check-in in two weeks.
3. Mom writes a diary entry about birth anxiety, brings only its title to her psychologist, and sees it land in her session brief. She books a session, adds her check-in summary to the brief and schedules it to go the day before; the PDF shows exactly what will be sent. The partner sees none of it.
4. Mom logs a mood check-in that completes a three-day low pattern. The plan updates and suggests a perinatal therapist from the directory, with the next real open time. She books it privately; it shows on her calendar, and she can move or cancel it.
5. Switch to the "newborn at home" family. The same engine now shows a completely different plan: postpartum visit, the baby's 2-month vaccines, the father's depression screen, night shifts and a handoff.
6. Open the health passport: labs, vaccines, care team and clinic in one place.
7. Open pricing: Free, Plus, Concierge.
8. Show the crisis button: fixed hotlines, no AI.
